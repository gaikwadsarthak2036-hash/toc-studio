What is an NFA?

Definition & key terms

Definition

A Non-deterministic Finite Automaton allows a state/symbol pair to lead to zero, one, or several next states.

How it's simulated

Instead of picking one path, the simulator tracks the whole set of states the machine could be in after each symbol.

Accept / Reject

The string is accepted if at least one state in the final active set is a final state.

Why it matters

Every NFA has an equivalent DFA (via the subset construction), but NFAs are often far easier to design by hand.
