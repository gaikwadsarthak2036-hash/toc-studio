What is a Turing Machine?

Definition & key terms

Definition

A Turing Machine reads and writes symbols on an infinite tape, moving its head left or right after every step, guided by a table of transition rules.

Halting

The machine halts when no rule matches the current state and symbol. It accepts if that happens in a designated final state.

Why it matters

Turing Machines are the theoretical model of what any computer can compute — more powerful than DFAs, NFAs, or PDAs.

How this simulator works

The tape starts with your input string and grows automatically with blank cells whenever the head moves past either end.
