What are Relations & Functions?

Definitions & key terms

Relation

A relation on a set A is simply a collection of ordered pairs (a, b) with a, b ∈ A — it doesn't need to follow any rule.

Reflexive, Symmetric, Transitive

Reflexive: every element relates to itself. Symmetric: (a,b) in R implies (b,a) in R. Transitive: (a,b) and (b,c) in R implies (a,c) in R.

Function

A function from A to B assigns exactly one output in B to every input in A — that's what makes it different from a general relation.

Injective, Surjective, Bijective

Injective: no two inputs share an output. Surjective: every element of B is hit. Bijective: both at once — and only then does an inverse function exist.
